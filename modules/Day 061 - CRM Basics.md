# Day 061 - CRM Basics

## Introduction

Customer Relationship Management (CRM) is a strategy and technology used by businesses to manage customer interactions, organize leads, improve customer satisfaction, and increase sales.

CRM systems help businesses track customer information, automate workflows, manage communication, and build long-term relationships.

Modern CRM platforms integrate:

* Sales
* Marketing
* Customer Support
* Analytics
* Automation
* Artificial Intelligence

In college marketing, CRM systems help manage:

* Admission inquiries
* Student follow-ups
* Parent communication
* Scholarship applications
* Webinar registrations
* Alumni relationships

---

# What is CRM?

CRM can be defined as:

> "A combination of strategies, processes, and software used to manage customer relationships throughout the customer lifecycle."

The goal of CRM is to:

* Improve customer satisfaction
* Increase sales
* Organize customer data
* Automate workflows
* Improve communication
* Build long-term relationships

---

# Why CRM is Important

CRM helps businesses:

* Centralize customer information
* Track leads efficiently
* Improve customer experience
* Increase conversion rates
* Automate repetitive tasks
* Improve team collaboration

Without CRM:

* Leads are lost
* Follow-ups are missed
* Customer information becomes scattered

---

# Evolution of CRM

### Stage 1: Manual CRM

Businesses used:

* Paper records
* Excel sheets
* Physical files

Problems:

* Difficult to manage
* Poor scalability

---

### Stage 2: Digital CRM

Businesses adopted:

* Contact databases
* Customer tracking software

Examples:

* Early Salesforce
* Microsoft CRM

---

### Stage 3: Cloud CRM

CRM moved to the cloud.

Benefits:

* Accessible anywhere
* Better collaboration
* Easy scalability

Examples:

* Salesforce
* HubSpot
* Zoho CRM

---

### Stage 4: AI-Powered CRM

Modern CRM systems use:

* AI Lead Scoring
* Predictive Analytics
* AI Chatbots
* Automation
* Customer Segmentation

Examples:

* Salesforce Einstein
* HubSpot AI
* Zoho Zia
