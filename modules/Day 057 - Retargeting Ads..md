# Day 057 - Retargeting Ads

## Introduction

Retargeting Ads are a powerful digital marketing strategy used to reconnect with people who have already interacted with a business but did not complete a desired action.

For example:

* A visitor opens a college admission page but does not apply.
* A user watches 75% of a webinar video but does not register.
* A customer adds a product to the cart but does not purchase.

Retargeting Ads help businesses show relevant ads to these users again and encourage them to complete the action.

Studies show that most users do not convert on their first visit. Retargeting helps marketers remind users about their interest and guide them back into the conversion funnel.

In college marketing, retargeting can be used for:

* Admission inquiries
* Webinar registrations
* Scholarship campaigns
* Campus visit reminders
* Course promotions

---

# What are Retargeting Ads?

Retargeting Ads can be defined as:

> "Advertisements shown to users who have previously interacted with a website, social media page, application, or advertisement but did not complete a desired action."

The goal is to:

* Reconnect users
* Increase conversions
* Build brand recall
* Reduce acquisition costs
* Improve ROI

---

# Why Retargeting is Important

Most users do not convert immediately.

Reasons include:

* Need more information
* Comparing alternatives
* Lack of urgency
* Busy schedule
* Need more trust

Retargeting helps businesses:

* Stay in customers' minds
* Increase brand awareness
* Improve conversion rates
* Recover lost leads
* Increase customer lifetime value

---

# Retargeting vs Remarketing

| Retargeting                    | Remarketing                      |
| ------------------------------ | -------------------------------- |
| Mostly uses ads                | Mostly uses email                |
| Targets website visitors       | Targets existing customers       |
| Uses Meta Pixel and Google Tag | Uses email automation            |
| Focuses on paid advertising    | Focuses on relationship building |

In practice, both terms are often used interchangeably.

# Types of Retargeting

---

## 1. Website Retargeting

Targets people who visited a website.

Examples:

* Visited admission page
* Viewed scholarship page
* Opened course page

Tools:

* Meta Pixel
* Google Tag

---

## 2. Social Media Retargeting

Targets users who interacted with:

* Instagram profile
* Facebook page
* Reels
* Videos
* Stories

Examples:

* Users who watched 50% of a video
* Users who liked a post
* Users who saved a reel

---

## 3. Email Retargeting

Targets users who:

* Opened email
* Clicked email link
* Registered but did not convert

Example:

Student downloads brochure but does not apply.

> Admissions closing soon. Apply today.

---

## 4. Search Retargeting

Targets users who searched for specific keywords.

Examples:

* Engineering colleges
* AI courses
* Admissions 2026

Ads are shown later through search or display networks.

---

## 5. Dynamic Retargeting

Shows personalized ads based on user behavior.

Example:

A student visits:

* AI Course page

Later sees:

> Admissions Open for AI Program.

Dynamic retargeting is highly personalized.

# Retargeting Strategy

A successful retargeting campaign requires planning.

---

## Audience Segmentation

Create separate audiences.

Example:

Audience 1

```text
Visited Admissions Page

Did Not Apply
```

Audience 2

```text
Watched Webinar

Did Not Register
```

Audience 3

```text
Downloaded Brochure

No Follow-Up Action
```

Different audiences require different messages.

---

## Frequency Capping

Avoid showing ads too many times.

Too many ads:

* Annoy users
* Increase costs
* Reduce effectiveness

Recommended:

3–7 impressions per week.

---

## Sequential Ads

Show ads in stages.

Example:

Ad 1

```text
Admissions Open
```

↓

Ad 2

```text
Explore AI and Engineering Programs
```

↓

Ad 3

```text
Apply Today
```

Sequential ads guide users through the funnel.

---

## Creative Personalization

Show ads based on user behavior.

Example:

User visits:

AI Course Page

Show:

> Learn AI and Build Real-World Projects.

Personalized ads often perform better.

# Important Retargeting Metrics

---

## CTR

Measures:

> Percentage of users clicking ads.

Formula:

\text{CTR}=\frac{\text{Clicks}}{\text{Impressions}}\times100

---

## CPC

Measures:

> Cost per click.

Formula:

\text{CPC}=\frac{\text{Ad Spend}}{\text{Clicks}}

---

## CPA

Measures:

> Cost per conversion.

Formula:

\text{CPA}=\frac{\text{Ad Spend}}{\text{Conversions}}

---

## ROAS

Measures:

> Return on advertising spend.

Formula:

\text{ROAS}=\frac{\text{Revenue}}{\text{Ad Spend}}

Higher ROAS means better campaign performance.
