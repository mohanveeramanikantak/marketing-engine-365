# Day 054 - Analytics Basics

## Introduction

Analytics is the process of collecting, measuring, analyzing, and interpreting data to understand performance and make better decisions. In digital marketing, analytics helps businesses understand customer behavior, evaluate campaign effectiveness, and improve marketing strategies.

Without analytics, marketers rely on assumptions. With analytics, marketers make decisions based on real data.

Businesses use analytics to answer questions such as:

* How many people visited the website?
* Which marketing channel generated the most leads?
* Why are visitors leaving the website?
* Which campaign generated the highest ROI?
* Which content performs best?

In college marketing, analytics can help track admission campaigns, webinar registrations, social media performance, landing page conversions, and student engagement.

# Types of Analytics

Analytics can be classified into four major categories.

---

## 1. Descriptive Analytics

Descriptive Analytics answers:

> What happened?

It summarizes historical data.

### Examples:

* Website received 20,000 visitors.
* Instagram gained 1,000 followers.
* Admission inquiries increased by 15%.

Tools:

* Google Analytics
* Excel dashboards
* Social media insights

---

## 2. Diagnostic Analytics

Diagnostic Analytics answers:

> Why did it happen?

It identifies reasons behind outcomes.

### Examples:

* Why did bounce rate increase?
* Why did webinar registrations drop?
* Why did Instagram reach increase?

Techniques:

* Segmentation
* Funnel analysis
* Root cause analysis

---

## 3. Predictive Analytics

Predictive Analytics answers:

> What is likely to happen?

It uses historical data and AI models.

### Examples:

* Predict future admissions.
* Forecast campaign performance.
* Predict customer behavior.

Techniques:

* Machine Learning
* Regression models
* Forecasting

---

## 4. Prescriptive Analytics

Prescriptive Analytics answers:

> What should we do?

It provides recommendations.

### Examples:

* Increase Instagram budget.
* Improve landing page CTA.
* Send personalized emails.

Prescriptive analytics combines data with AI recommendations.

# Marketing Metrics

Metrics are measurable values used to evaluate marketing performance.

---

## Users

Users represent unique visitors.

Example:

If 1,000 people visit a website:

Users = 1,000

---

## Sessions

A session is a group of interactions by a user within a specific period.

Example:

One user visits three times:

Users = 1
Sessions = 3

---

## Page Views

Total number of pages viewed.

Example:

A visitor opens:

* Homepage
* Course page
* Admission page

Page Views = 3

---

## Bounce Rate

Bounce Rate measures visitors leaving without interaction.

Formula:

\text{Bounce Rate} = \frac{\text{Single Page Sessions}}{\text{Total Sessions}} \times 100

Lower bounce rate is usually better.

---

## Average Session Duration

Measures how long users stay on the website.

Longer duration often indicates:

* Better engagement
* Useful content
* Good user experience

---

## Click Through Rate (CTR)

CTR measures the percentage of users who click a link.

\text{CTR} = \frac{\text{Clicks}}{\text{Impressions}} \times 100

Example:

* Impressions = 10,000
* Clicks = 500

CTR = 5%

---

## Conversion Rate

Measures the percentage of users who complete a desired action.

\text{Conversion Rate} = \frac{\text{Conversions}}{\text{Visitors}} \times 100

Examples:

* Admission inquiry
* Purchase
* Registration
* Download

---

## Cost Per Click (CPC)

Measures advertising cost per click.

Formula:

\text{CPC} = \frac{\text{Total Ad Spend}}{\text{Total Clicks}}

Lower CPC generally means more efficient advertising.

---

## Cost Per Acquisition (CPA)

Measures the cost required to acquire a lead or customer.

Formula:

\text{CPA} = \frac{\text{Marketing Cost}}{\text{Total Conversions}}

---

## Return on Investment (ROI)

ROI measures profitability.

Formula:

\text{ROI} = \frac{\text{Profit} - \text{Investment}}{\text{Investment}} \times 100

Higher ROI indicates better campaign performance.
