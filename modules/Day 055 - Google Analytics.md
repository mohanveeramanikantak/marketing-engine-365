## 🧠 Why This Matters

Google Analytics helps businesses understand:

* Who visits the website
* Where visitors come from
* What pages they visit
* How long they stay
* Which campaigns perform best
* Where users drop off

👉 Effective use of Google Analytics helps businesses:

* Improve website performance
* Increase conversions
* Optimize marketing campaigns
* Reduce bounce rates
* Improve user experience
* Make data-driven decisions

In college marketing:

* Admission campaigns
* Webinar registrations
* Landing pages
* Scholarship campaigns
* Social media traffic

can all be measured and improved using Google Analytics.
