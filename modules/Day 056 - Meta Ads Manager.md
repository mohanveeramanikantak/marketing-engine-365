# Important Advertising Metrics

---

## Reach

Number of unique people who saw the ad.

---

## Impressions

Total number of times the ad was displayed.

---

## Click Through Rate (CTR)

Measures:

> Percentage of users clicking the ad.

Formula:

\text{CTR} = \frac{\text{Clicks}}{\text{Impressions}} \times 100

Higher CTR generally indicates more attractive ads.

---

## Cost Per Click (CPC)

Measures:

> Cost of each click.

Formula:

\text{CPC} = \frac{\text{Ad Spend}}{\text{Clicks}}

---

## Cost Per Acquisition (CPA)

Measures:

> Cost required to acquire a lead or customer.

Formula:

\text{CPA} = \frac{\text{Ad Spend}}{\text{Conversions}}

---

## CPM

CPM stands for:

> Cost Per Thousand Impressions.

Formula:

\text{CPM} = \frac{\text{Ad Spend}}{\text{Impressions}} \times 1000

---

## Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)

Measures:

> Revenue generated for every rupee spent.

Formula:

\text{ROAS} = \frac{\text{Revenue}}{\text{Ad Spend}}

Higher ROAS means better campaign performance.
